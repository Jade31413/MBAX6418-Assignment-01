Emotion pairs and scoring rules

NRC counts exact matching words in the title and body. Repeated words count again. Ties use alphabetical order; every tied maximum is retained. “None” means no supported emotion or no NRC emotional match, not neutral sentiment. NRC does not interpret negation or sarcasm.

Method & sources

Ratings are the reference

Balanced run: 1–2 stars negative, 3 neutral, 4–5 positive. First-100 run: 1–3 negative, 4–5 positive. Only review titles and text enter the model; rating metadata is reserved for evaluation.

A repeatable sample

50 reviews per class are sampled across the entire dataset without replacement. Seed: 6418. Raw responses and exact prompts are saved. Metrics reproduce from saved responses; fresh model calls may vary.

Two ways to read emotion

Qwen interprets context. NRC sums word associations across eight emotions. Both can return “none.” Word-list ties and no-match cases are shown explicitly.
